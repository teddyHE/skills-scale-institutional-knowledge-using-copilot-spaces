# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme uses a customer-focused, iterative project lifecycle that begins with initiation and ends with continuous improvement. Initiation validates the business need, success measures, stakeholders, timeline, resources, and initial risks. Approved work moves into planning, where the team creates a prioritized, estimable backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, dependencies, and a release plan.

Delivery is tracked through a project board and small pull requests that link to issues and acceptance criteria. The Project Manager coordinates schedules, risks, dependencies, resources, and communications; the Product Manager defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success; developers implement and test the solution; and QA/testing validates quality and acceptance. Stakeholders provide input and approvals, while clear ownership and psychological safety support transparent, data-informed decisions.

OctoAcme maintains predictable communication through daily standups, weekly delivery or PM syncs, milestone demos, and monthly stakeholder updates. A single source of truth holds project plans, status, risks, and decisions. Blockers are escalated progressively from team-level triage to the PM and Product Lead, dependent teams or specialists, and sponsors or incident responders when business or security impact requires it.

Quality is embedded throughout delivery. Teams use unit and integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, manual QA when needed, CI tests and security scans, and required review before merging. Releases verify acceptance criteria, staging and production deployment steps, rollback plans, monitoring, and stakeholder announcements. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, or incidents identify lessons and convert prioritized improvements into owned, dated action items.
